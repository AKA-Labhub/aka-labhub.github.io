Sort Café – Privacy Policy

By AKA Labs · Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: Sort Café has no accounts and we (AKA Labs) do not collect your personal information. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

1. Who we are

Sort Café (“the game”) is made by AKA Labs, an independent developer based in Bangladesh. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at syedantor7@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your phone, so you can continue where you left off:

  • your current level and coins;
  • whether you claimed today’s Daily Treat;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration).

This information is not sent to us and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone’s settings. If you use Android’s own device backup, your phone may back it up to your Google account under Google’s terms.

3. Information we collect

AKA Labs does not ask for, collect, store or sell your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. The game has no sign-in and no chat.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including banner ads, ads between levels, and optional reward videos (for example, to get a hint or extra coins). To show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (e.g. country);
  • device and app information, such as phone model, operating system and app version;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps) and diagnostic information such as crash data.

Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalised (based on your interests) or non-personalised. Google’s use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: when you first open the game, you’ll be asked for your consent to the use of data for ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone’s settings under Google → Ads (the exact menu may vary by phone).
  • Stop all data collection: you can stop all collection by the game by uninstalling it.

6. Children

Sort Café is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data held on our side. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google’s security practices.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example if we add new features. The “Last updated” date at the top will show when it last changed. Please check this page from time to time.
